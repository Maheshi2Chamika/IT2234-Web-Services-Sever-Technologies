---------------------To find first output---------------------------------------

------------------Finding students who are elder than 22---------------------

{age:{$gt:22}}                                    //mongodb command

db.student.find({"age":{$gt:22}})




------------------Finding students who knows mongodb---------------------

{skills:{$in:["Mongodb"]}}                        //mongodb command

db.student.find({"skills":"Mongodb"})


------------------Finding students who knows mongodb & java---------------------


{skills:{$in:["Mongodb","Java"]}}                 //mongodb command



------------------Finding students who are not in mongodb & java---------------------

{skills:{$nin:["Mongodb","Java"]}}                //mongodb command


-----------------Sorting according to gpa----------------------------
//using mongodb
{gpa:1}   //Ascending order
{gpa:-1}  //Descending order


db.student.find().sort({'gpa':1})    // using command shell



------------------IT students Sorted by gpa----------------------------

db.student.find({'degree':'IT'}).sort({'gpa':1})



-----------------Male IT students sort by their age--------------------

db.student.find({'gender':'Male'}).sort({'age':1})


-----------------Multiple conditions-----------------------------------

db.student.find({'gender':'Male','degree':'IT'}).sort({'age':1})
